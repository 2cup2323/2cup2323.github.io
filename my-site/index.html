Status Guide

Learn what each status means, including server availability and whether a domain is accessible from your current network.

Server Status:Shows whether the server behind the domain is online globally.

Your Status:Shows whether your browser, network, or country can access the domain.

Online:
The server behind the domain is responding normally.
Accessible:
You can reach this domain from your current browser or network.
Down:
The domain is not responding or may be under maintenance.
Blocked:
Your browser or network could not reach this domain. Your internet provider, network, or country may be restricting access to it. Try another WatchLuna domain.
